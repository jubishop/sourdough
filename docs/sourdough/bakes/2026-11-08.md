## November 8, 2026 — Temperature standardization decision (planned, not yet tested)

**Decision for the upcoming bake (revised later November 8):** Use a fixed proofing-box setting of **76°F**, not a flexible "74–78°F" range, and retain the user-approved **1 US quart (~946 mL) mark at the glass** for stopping bulk. Earlier in the discussion, the user tentatively chose 74°F, then explicitly changed the next-bake target to **76°F**; the 74°F plan is superseded. The user values a repeatable, *fixed* proof-box temperature because the dough's surface shape changes substantially with temperature: at approximately **78°F** the dough makes a pronounced dome, whereas at **74°F** the surface is flatter. Thus the same height against the glass does **not necessarily** mean the same total dough volume or stage of fermentation at these two temperatures. The user chooses **not** to recalibrate the one-quart endpoint before this bake; record both the set temperature and observed shape. This is a working selection for the next bake, **not** evidence that 76°F improves loaf quality or that any published recipe/code was edited.

**Experimental caveat:** The prior November 7 plan also specified switching from four fold sessions to two (T+30 and T+60 minutes). If both changes are used in the same upcoming bake, differences from the preceding loaf cannot be attributed to temperature alone. Do not infer results until the loaf is baked and assessed.

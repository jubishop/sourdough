# 2026-11-06 — Pre-proof observation

## November 6, 2026 — No pre-shaping chill; very large belly button (pre-bake)

**Actual variable changes:** The user stopped bulk at **1 US quart (~946 mL)**, versus **1.0 L** on recent bakes (approximately 5.4% less final volume); removed the pre-shaping refrigeration; and shaped solely by hand-cupping, **without any countertop tension-building pulls**. These combined changes mean finished-loaf differences cannot be isolated to one variable. **Observed:** The user removed the pre-shaping refrigeration entirely, yet the dough developed a particularly large, deep underside cavity (“belly button”) with multiple overlapping folds gathered toward the middle. It was photographed after the dough was placed seam-side up in the banneton, before baking. The dough felt quite firm at shaping time. **User explicitly did no countertop pulling at all**: instead, they gently cupped the dough with their hands to shape it, hoping to prevent the defect. A large belly button formed anyway. The user reported the dough still **released readily from the container without chilling** and chose to retain no pre-shape chill as a permanent baseline improvement. The 1 US quart bulk endpoint and shaping method remain trials, not accepted baseline changes.

[Pre-proof photo of the underside seam in the banneton](../photos/2026-11-06-belly-button-preproof.jpeg).

**Interpretation:** Refrigeration is *not necessary* for the belly-button defect to appear. Neither refrigeration nor countertop pulling is necessary for this defect. The hand-cupping motion might have gathered the underside, or folds might have arisen during earlier handling or container release. These are unverified possibilities; no completed crumb/bake result from this loaf is recorded yet.

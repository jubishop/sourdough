**Next deliberate experiment: fewer folds with more rest**

- Evaluate extensibility/tightness when shaping, loaf height and oven spring, score opening/tearing, crust, and whether crumb becomes more open/airy or instead spreads/weakens. More airy crumb and less aggressive tearing are **hypotheses**, not guaranteed outcomes.

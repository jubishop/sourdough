**Status: BAKED Saturday, November 14; photos saved, the user's assessment not recorded.** Decision made Thursday, November 12, for a bake planned on Friday, November 13; the user baked it on November 14. This updates the next-bake plan recorded in [the November 12 assessment](2026-11-12.md). The user confirmed the loaf used King Arthur Golden Wheat; see [Outcome](#outcome--november-14).

## Purpose and changes

Try **King Arthur Golden Wheat (hard white, 100% whole-wheat flour; orange bag)** instead of the prior **Bob's Red Mill Organic Stone Ground Whole Wheat (hard red wheat)**. Use a **60-minute flour-and-water autolyse**, as chosen after considering whole-wheat hydration and unverified falling-number information. This first trial changes **both flour and autolyse time** compared with the recently evaluated Bob's loaf (15-minute autolyse); it is a practical new-flour trial, **not** an isolated, single-variable comparison. Compared with the former habitual ~90-minute autolyse, 60 minutes is shorter.

## Planned formula and controls (record actual values after baking)

- **Autolyse:** 500 g **King Arthur Golden Wheat** flour and 400 g water; covered rest **60 minutes**, without levain or salt.
- **Then add:** 100 g ripe **100%-hydration levain** and 12 g salt. The resulting formula remains **550 g total flour / 450 g total water = 81.8% calculated hydration**, excluding unmeasured wet-hand water.
- **Bulk:** target **1.0 L at the glass edge**, the user's current preferred endpoint. Record actual proof-box and dough temperatures, rise time and dough shape instead of assuming a particular temperature.
- **Everything else:** keep the current recipe's **three squash-and-fold rounds**, dough handling, shaping, no pre-shape chill, overnight cold proof, loading, and cooling unchanged as practicable; document actual departures. At the user's request on November 12, both site recipes use a 1-hour autolyse, and the autolyse step names each recipe's dough flour: Bob's Red Mill Organic Whole Wheat Flour in the Baseline and King Arthur Golden Whole Wheat Flour in the Experiment, and the Experiment's starter build also uses King Arthur Golden, so the dough flour is about 99% Golden Wheat. The mother refresh and weekly care still say only "flour". On November 13, the user changed the Experiment's bake to **20 minutes covered at 465°F** with no 390°F stretch, then **50 minutes uncovered at 315°F**, for a 70-minute bake (the Baseline is 10 minutes at 465°F and 20 at 390°F covered, then 45 minutes uncovered at 315°F). This adds a third change versus the recent Bob's loaf.
- **Levain carryover:** The mother starter is still based on Bob's. **Confirmed by the user on November 12:** to minimize Bob's in this loaf, build 130 g of levain from **10 g Bob's mother starter + 60 g King Arthur Golden Wheat flour + 60 g water**, then use 100 g of the build. Assuming a 100%-hydration mother, that introduces only **~3.85 g Bob's flour, ~0.7% of 550 g total flour**. If the 100 g levain is made entirely with Bob's, that supplies **50 g Bob's flour (~9.1%)**. The site's Experiment starter step now says to add 60 g water + 60 g King Arthur Golden Whole Wheat Flour. Record the flour actually used.

## What to assess once baked

The goals, in order, remain (1) **less rubbery/chewy mouthfeel** after full cooling, rather than just lower oven moisture; (2) **lighter, more evenly open crumb** away from any isolated shaping cavity; (3) **more vertical oven spring** with less spread. Record how new flour absorbs water at the unchanged nominal hydration, dough tension/extensibility, any underside "belly button", crumb pore distribution, loaf height/score opening, photos and actual fermentation/bake/cooling details. If a flour benefit is seen, repeat before declaring it a baseline improvement.

**Causal limitation:** King Arthur white whole wheat changes more than one flour characteristic. The actual falling number for the user's retail bag is **not confirmed**, and any crumb change cannot be assigned specifically to amylase or to the changed autolyse time.

# 2026-11-17 — limited oven spring and reassessment of covered bake time

**Status: baked November 17; user observations and photo review recorded.** Exterior and crumb images were shared in chat, but the user will add the photographs to GitHub separately. No photo files or links are committed here.

## Reference formula and recipe context

The published Baseline and Experiment recipes currently both specify **500 g King Arthur Golden Whole Wheat Flour, 400 g main-dough water, 100 g 100%-hydration levain and 12 g salt**: **550 g total flour / 450 g total water = 81.8% calculated hydration**. All oven temperatures in the recipes are **dial settings**; the documented oven runs about **15°F cooler than the dial**.

The published recipes' bake schedule when this loaf was discussed was:

| Stage | Dial setting | Duration |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Covered | 465°F | 20 min |
| Covered | 390°F | 10 min |
| Uncovered | 315°F | 45 min |
| **Total** | | **75 min** |

**Confirmed for this bake:** The user reported **30 minutes total covered** before uncovering. The stage temperatures and full uncovered duration above are the **site recipe reference**, not independently confirmed measurements for this particular bake. The center temperature was not reported.

**Other recipe differences, not verified as actual bake settings:** The current Experiment recipe uses **no autolyse**, water/levain/salt paddle-mixed before flour, **four** squash-and-fold rounds, and a **1.0 L glass-edge bulk endpoint**; the Baseline retains a 60-minute autolyse, three rounds, and a **1 US quart glass-edge endpoint**. Both recipes use two perforated silicone liners plus a flat circular King Arthur liner under the parchment. The user has not separately confirmed which of these settings was actually followed for this finished loaf. Do not treat them as measured actuals or claim this bake isolated a single variable.

## Observations

- **Vertical oven spring was disappointing.** The exterior looked reasonably baked with visibly opened scoring, but the loaf had limited height compared with the desired taller shape.
- **At lid removal after 30 minutes covered, the loaf still did not look substantially sprung.** The extra covered time did not deliver the obvious covered-phase expansion the user had hoped for.
- **Apparent later expansion may have been an illusion.** The user initially thought the loaf grew chiefly after uncovering, then agreed that the scored sections spreading apart and lifting could have made it look that way. There was no separate measurement of whole-loaf height or volume at lid removal and later, so do **not** conclude that most true oven spring happened uncovered.
- **Crumb in the shared cross-section photo:** a fairly tight pattern of predominantly small-to-medium holes, a few larger irregular pockets, and some apparent compression/density toward the base. It is not yet as open and airy as desired. A photo cannot establish the texture goal (rubbery/chewy/gummy mouthfeel); the user did not give a new tasting assessment for this loaf.
- **Comparison with November 14:** that loaf also had little obvious rise when uncovered after 20 minutes. Neither observation demonstrates that a longer covered interval improves spring. The baking comparisons also include other recipe and process changes.

## Interpretation — hypotheses, not established causes

- **Longer covered time did not visibly solve the low-spring problem.** A further covered-time extension is not presently justified by these observations.
- Opening of the scores must be distinguished from actual increases in the loaf's overall height or volume. If possible, compare loaf profile at loading, at lid removal, and at completion on a future bake.
- Dough mixing/strength and fermentation are plausible factors in a tight crumb and low vertical spring; neither is established as the primary cause. On November 16 the paddle failed to incorporate levain/salt into already-autolysed dough; the site's new no-autolyse procedure aims to address that **mixing workflow**. Its actual success for this loaf has not been documented.
- The **1-quart bulk endpoint may be conservative**, but this remains a testable question, not a conclusion. The site's Experiment track already specifies **1.0 L** (about 5.7% above 1 US quart), while the Baseline specifies **1 quart**. Actual bulk endpoint, dough temperature, and cold-proof length for this bake have not been recorded. Avoid attributing the result to under- or over-fermentation without those values.

## Accepted next-bake plan — November 17

Shorten the bake by removing the final **10-minute covered 390°F phase**, rather than lengthening it further:

| Stage | Dial setting | Duration |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Covered | **465°F** | **20 min** |
| Uncovered | **315°F** | **45 min** |
| **Total planned** | | **65 min** |

- At the end of the 45-minute uncovered stage, **measure the center temperature**, aiming for approximately **208°F**. This is a **future target**, not a measurement from today's loaf.
- If the center is **below about 205°F**, continue uncovered at 315°F for **5 minutes** and recheck. If it is **205–208°F**, remove the loaf; avoid extending baking simply to chase the rubbery/gummy mouthfeel, which previous long-bake tests did not solve.
- Retain the current flour, nominal hydration, levain, salt, and bottom-protection arrangement unless otherwise deliberately changed. Record the **actual** autolyse/mixing method, fold count, glass-edge bulk endpoint, proofing temperature, cold-proof duration, and center temperature, especially since the Experiment and Baseline currently differ on several of those parameters.
- This changes both **total bake duration** (75 → 65 minutes, before any temperature-driven extension) and the **390°F covered interval**, so it is **not** a lid-timing-only controlled experiment. Assess final internal temperature, crust and crumb as well as height and score expansion.
- **Documentation decision only:** The user asked to update the notes in GitHub, **not** to edit the deployed Baseline or Experiment recipe. Those recipes remain unchanged pending a separate instruction.

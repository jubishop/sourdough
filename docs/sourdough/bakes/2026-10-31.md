**Observed:** Strong oven spring; crumb slightly too moist but no longer egregiously gummy; bottom fully baked and not scorched; deep central underside “belly-button” cavity that corresponds to a compressed internal fold. Photo evidence favors forceful countertop shaping/gathered seam rather than gross fermentation failure (hypothesis, not proof).

**Known handling:** The dough was chilled when bulk reached about 1.0 L at the glass edge; pre-shape chill had been about 60 minutes to ease releasing dough from the vessel. The agreed next-bake experiment was 45 minutes chill and gentler/fewer tightening pulls, holding other variables fixed. The later November 3 site actually used a 15-minute chill.

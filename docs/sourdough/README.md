## Current conversational decisions (November 11, 2026)

- Priority goals: (1) reduce perceived crumb gumminess, (2) improve open and even crumb, (3) improve vertical oven spring.
- Next autolyse comparison: 15 minutes versus historical 90 minutes; outcome not yet recorded in this migration.
- If 15 minutes is better or essentially equivalent, retain the shorter autolyse for future tests. Next candidate: slightly earlier bulk endpoint; hydration reduction is another high-leverage candidate.
- Two further backlog proposals: hotter covered bake start (475–500°F proposed) and scalding 15–20% of flour. These are untested hypotheses, not adopted instructions.
- Excessive amylase activity is a hypothesis for gumminess, **not an established diagnosis**. Scalding can deactivate some enzymes if the flour-water mixture becomes sufficiently hot, but also gelatinizes starch and may increase perceived moisture.
- Keep total flour at 550 g and total water at 450 g (81.8% nominal hydration) when changing the quantity of 100%-hydration levain, unless hydration itself is the experimental variable.

## Working method

Log actual ingredient weights, times, temperatures, bulk endpoint, proof duration, observations, and exterior/crumb images. Separate observations from hypotheses. Change one variable per bake where feasible. Promote a successful experiment to Baseline only after explicit user approval, ideally following repeat bakes. Reference the Git commit of the recipe used when possible.

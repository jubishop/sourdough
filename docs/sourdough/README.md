## Latest working decisions — November 14, 2026

1. Goal order stays **less rubbery/gummy-feeling crumb**, then **larger and more evenly distributed air pockets**, then **vertical oven spring**. User clarified the unwanted crumb texture is mainly **rubbery and chewy**, not paste-like/sticky.
2. **15-minute autolyse experiment assessed November 12:** No improvement in gumminess or noticeable shaping-time dough handling compared with the 90-minute experience. A huge localized cavity and the recurring underside belly button were seen, but **crumb near the outer edges away from the shaping defect looked fairly good**. Aggressive shaping is a plausible cause of the localized void, not a proven reason for the general rubbery texture. [Full observation](bakes/2026-11-12.md).
3. **Golden Wheat trial (planned for November 13, baked November 14; results recorded):** King Arthur **Golden Wheat hard-white 100% whole wheat** replaces the usual **Bob's Red Mill Organic Stone Ground hard-red whole wheat**, with a **60-minute autolyse**. Keep nominal **81.8% hydration**, **100 g levain**, **12 g salt**, **1.0 L glass-edge bulk target**, three fold rounds and the remaining recipe process unchanged. Both flour and autolyse differ from the previous 15-minute Bob's test, so this is not a one-variable comparison. The user confirmed on November 12 that the overnight levain build is fed King Arthur Golden Wheat, limiting Bob's carryover from the mother to about 0.7% of total flour. See [November 14 Golden Wheat bake](bakes/2026-11-14.md). Earlier zero- and 2.5–3-hour autolyse plans were not baked.
   **Baked outcome:** User prefers Golden Wheat and found the crumb **less gummy but still somewhat gummy**; crumb photo shows reasonably uniform small/medium holes and reduced bottom compression, though openness remains below target. **At 20 minutes, just before uncovering, almost no oven spring was visible; most came during uncovered baking, with less total spring than earlier loaves.** Full actual bake settings beyond the 20-minute lid removal were not separately confirmed. See [November 14 results and next plan](bakes/2026-11-14.md).
4. Current formula reference: **550 g total whole-wheat flour, 450 g total water, 12 g salt, 100 g 100%-hydration levain** (nominal 81.8% hydration). Lowering hydration made the dough difficult to work, and extended uncovered baking has not fixed the texture. Do not prioritize either.
5. **72°F** is a recently documented bulk-temperature setting, but the **actual setting for the November 14 Golden Wheat bake was not recorded**. Golden Wheat's retail falling number has not been established; excessive amylase in Bob's remains an unproven hypothesis. Record actual levain feed flour, as the mother starter still uses Bob's.
6. These are research-journal updates only: **do not change the deployed Baseline or Experiment recipe** by inference. At the user's request on November 12, both site recipes use a 1-hour autolyse. On November 14, the user promoted the Experiment to the Baseline, so both recipes now name King Arthur Golden Whole Wheat Flour in the autolyse and the starter build, making the dough flour about 99% Golden Wheat, and bake 20 minutes covered at 465°F, then 50 minutes uncovered at 315°F. The mother refresh and weekly care still say only "flour". The agreed **next bake** changes mixing to the stand-mixer **paddle** (all 400 g water in the autolyse; levain and salt added together, no bassinage or additional kneading) and aims for **20 minutes covered at 465°F, 10 minutes covered at 390°F, then 45 minutes uncovered at 315°F** (75 minutes total; target ~208°F internal). Later on November 14, at the user's request, both site recipes began mixing with the paddle, without naming a speed, and the Experiment added **10 minutes covered at 390°F** after the 20 minutes at 465°F, shortened the uncovered bake to **45 minutes** for a 75-minute bake matching the plan, and stacked **two perforated silicone liners** on the loaf lifter instead of one. The user then promoted these Experiment changes to the Baseline the same day, so both site recipes match. The user then had both site recipes note the ~208°F internal target and set the bulk target to exactly 1 qt at the glass edge, replacing the 1 qt to 1 L range.

7. **Hydration experiment split (November 14):** [Experiment #4](experiments.md#experiment-04-active) now tests **higher hydration only** (420 g main-dough water upfront; 85.5% overall) versus the 400 g / 81.8% control, with **no bassinage**. ## Latest planning decision — November 16, 2026

- **Next planned test: no autolyse**, under existing [Experiment #1](experiments.md#next-planned-trial--experiment-1-no-autolyse-november-16-2026). The stand-mixer paddle could not incorporate levain and salt after autolyse because the dough formed a rotating clump. Next time, disperse 100 g levain and 12 g salt in the 400 g water first, then paddle-mix in 500 g King Arthur Golden Wheat flour until homogeneous. No autolyse or extra kneading; keep 81.8% hydration.
- **Initial rest selected: 30 minutes**, measured from levain incorporation; then three planned squash-and-fold sessions at T+30, +60 and +90. A fourth at +120 was suggested as a possibility but not decided at first; the user later chose it (next bullet). The aim is to finish folds earlier in fermentation.
- **Experiment recipe updated (later November 16):** at the user's request, the site's Experiment recipe now has no autolyse (water, levain and salt paddle-mixed first, then flour added immediately) and **exactly four** squash-and-fold rounds, 30 minutes before each. The Baseline keeps the 1-hour autolyse and three rounds. Four rounds plus no autolyse changes two variables at once.
- **Liner stack changed in both recipes (November 17):** at the user's request, the Baseline and the Experiment add a **plain, flat, circular King Arthur silicone liner** on top of the two perforated liners, under the parchment. The next loaf therefore also differs from the November 14 loaf in bottom insulation, not only in autolyse and fold count.
- **Experiment bulk target changed (November 17):** at the user's request, the Experiment now aims for **exactly 1 L at the glass edge**, about 5% more than the Baseline's exactly 1 qt. The Experiment now differs from the Baseline in autolyse, fold count and bulk endpoint.
- **Experiment promoted to the Baseline (later November 17):** at the user's request, the Baseline now matches the Experiment: **no autolyse** (water, levain and salt paddle-mixed first, then flour), **exactly four** squash-and-fold rounds, and **exactly 1 L at the glass edge**. ## Latest bake outcome and next-bake decision — November 17, 2026

- **Observed:** The new loaf had disappointing vertical oven spring and a fairly tight crumb. Even after **30 minutes covered**, it did not look substantially risen at lid removal. Opening of the scored sections after uncovering may have **created an illusion** of further rise; actual whole-loaf growth after uncovering was not measured. Extending the covered interval from 20 to 30 minutes has shown **no convincing spring benefit**, but these bakes were not controlled single-variable comparisons.
- **Next-bake decision:** Return to **20 minutes covered at 465°F, followed immediately by 45 minutes uncovered at 315°F** (**65 minutes total planned**). Omit the additional 10-minute covered segment at 390°F. Optionally measure the loaf's **center temperature** with a thermometer probe as soon as it is out of the oven, aiming for **at least 208°F**. A low reading informs the **next** bake; the user does not return the loaf to the oven or extend its bake (clarified later November 17, superseding a rule to bake 5 more minutes below about 205°F). The target is not a reported measured result for today's loaf. Continue assessing dough mixing and fermentation, including whether 1 qt is a conservative bulk endpoint; those causes remain unproven.
- **Applied to both recipes (later November 17):** at the user's request, the Baseline and the Experiment now bake **20 min covered at 465°F, then 45 min uncovered at 315°F**, dropping the 10-minute covered 390°F stretch. The recipes' cooling step now gives the optional out-of-oven probe check, with any shortfall below 208°F adjusting the next bake. See [November 17 bake notes](bakes/2026-11-17.md), which include the exterior and crumb photos.

## Latest bake outcome and working plan — November 19, 2026

- **Actual November 19 oven results:** 20 minutes **covered at 465°F** and 45 minutes **uncovered at 315°F** yielded **205°F center**, short of the user's **208°F** target. An additional 15 minutes **uncovered at 315°F** reached the target (80 minutes total). The precise final temperature was not reported, and the user suspects that not all the extra time was necessary. **Next bake confirmed by the user:** **20 minutes covered at 465°F + 10 minutes covered at 390°F + 45 minutes uncovered at 315°F** (75 minutes total), with one end-of-bake temperature check if desired. This differs from today's actual 20/60 schedule and has not yet been proven to reach 208°F.
- **Significant fermentation confounder:** Starter, levain **and bulk** were unusually slow; the user is refreshing the starter roughly every 12 hours. The user carefully observed **real loaf expansion after uncovering at minute 20**, not merely score cuts spreading; exact timing is unknown. Slow fermentation may limit spring, but is **not established to delay its timing**. Despite the fermentation confounder, the user elected to restore **10 minutes covered at 390°F** on the next bake; keep other experimental changes on hold while starter vigor recovers.
- **Crumb review:** Mostly tight, small pores with a few larger holes and mild basal density, below desired openness. No user tasting assessment yet: photo cannot establish gumminess. See the [November 19 bake record](bakes/2026-11-19.md).
- **Recipe history clarified:** At this update, `lib/recipes/baseline.ts` describes the confirmed **next-bake 20/10/45 schedule**, despite the historical November 17 note about an earlier site change to 20/45. **No deployed recipe was altered in this documentation update.**

## Latest bake outcome and fermentation priority — November 21, 2026

- **Bulk endpoint:** The user clarified that the actual November 21 bake went **closer to 1.0 L at the glass edge**, rather than merely 1 US quart (~946 mL). This is an approximate actual observation, not a newly selected endpoint or confirmation of exactly 1.0 L.
- **Oven:** Following the intended **20 min covered at 465°F + 10 min covered at 390°F + 45 min uncovered at 315°F** profile (75 min), the user saw **some expansion already present when uncovering**. The loaf was not markedly better overall; the X-score opened substantially, but meaningful extra *vertical* spring has not been established. We cannot tell whether the extra covered 10 minutes caused the expansion. **Internal temperature was not measured**; the user suspects at least 208°F, but that is unverified.
- **Crumb / texture:** Fine, tight crumb with scattered bigger upper pockets, a denser lower band, and **still a bit gummy after completely cooling**. The baker reports that fully cooling before slicing is always standard practice. This rules out premature slicing, while the history of extended baking makes insufficient oven time a less compelling leading explanation (without proving every loaf fully baked).
- **Next priority:** The user agrees **fermentation is the main variable to investigate next**, given recent sluggish starter, levain, and bulk behavior. Record actual starter vigor, dough temperature, elapsed bulk time, dome/volume cues and endpoint; consider a controlled later-endpoint trial only when vigor is stable. **No new endpoint, hydration, or baking schedule has been approved and neither deployed recipe was changed.** See [November 21 bake notes](bakes/2026-11-21.md), which include the exterior and crumb photos.

## Documentation rules

- Add or update experiment plans in **experiments.md**, not separate numbered files.
- Create or update **`bakes/YYYY-MM-DD.md`** for each dated bake. Put the actual recipe parameters, observations, photos, outcome, and follow-up decisions in that single file. A date with only a planning decision or pre-proof observation is explicitly labeled as such; do not invent a completed bake. Link photographs and recipe Git commits when available.
- **Do not recreate `bake-journal.md` or duplicate detailed bake notes in a running journal.** The dated records are the canonical historical source; use this README only as a navigation and current-status overview.
- Distinguish **observed results**, **planned changes**, and **hypotheses**. A later dated decision overrides an older plan, but not the historical record of what was actually baked.
- Prefer one controlled variable per bake; repeat promising results before promoting to Baseline.
- Start every page other than a README with `status: current` frontmatter, as [the page format](../README.md#page-format) describes, and link each new page from this README. `bin/check --documents-only` enforces both, and pushes to `main` fail without them.

# Bottom crust protection

The aluminum foil base is a saved option if the bottom of the loaf becomes
too dark. It is no longer part of the active recipe.

## Reason for removal

On 2026-09-15, the user asked to remove the foil base because they think
the silicone bread sling may provide enough protection for the bottom.
They explicitly asked to save the foil method because they may want to
bring it back. The sling's protection without foil has not yet been
established for this loaf and equipment. Reconsider the foil if a future
bake shows excessive bottom darkening.

## Former foil method

- While the Dutch oven is cold, loosely crumple a long sheet of aluminum
  foil, then shape and flatten it into a pad that fits the bottom.
- Keep the pad about ¼ inch thick and its top level. Start with a sheet
  larger than the bottom; flatten it enough to level the top while
  retaining the pad's thickness.
- Cover only the flat bottom of the user's shallow double Dutch oven.
  The former method did not include foil up the sides or greasing.
- Leave the pad in place during preheating and baking. Use the silicone
  sling to lower the scored loaf onto the pad.

If the user chooses to restore it, update the preheat step, schedule
reminder, and loading instruction together. Keep the separate pizza-stone
setup and baking settings aligned with the
[recipe guide](../docs/recipe-guide.md#preheat-the-pizza-stone-with-the-dutch-oven).
